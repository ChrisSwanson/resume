# Employer buildings: what each model is based on

Each employer building in `hq.py` is a simplified miniature of a real building. Photos were used
only for research. None are in the repo, and none are used as textures. Footprint proportions and
storey counts come from the sources below, scaled to fit the block each building stands in.

| id | Building | One-line description | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| tivo | 2160 (and 2190) Gold St, Alviso, San Jose CA: TiVo's long-time HQ campus | Low white two-storey office blocks with ribbon windows, sunshade louvres and a rounded corner entry, around palm-lined parking. | https://www.connectcre.com/stories/tivo-san-jose-hq-sold-for-92m/ |
| palantir | SugarCube, 1555 Blake St, Denver CO: the HQ from 2020 (KPMB, 2008) | Ten-storey corner block with a brick base and a lighter upper block stepped back above it, next to the 1906 Sugar Building. | https://denverarchitecture.org/site/sugar-cube/ |
| twitch | 350 Bush St, San Francisco CA (Heller Manus, 2018) | Nineteen-storey green-glass tower on a podium, behind the preserved 1923 Mining Exchange temple front with its columns. | https://www.hellermanus.com/350-bush |
| wework | 115 W 18th St, Chelsea, New York: the HQ through 2019 | Broad eight-storey 1917 loft block in the street wall, with a flat roof, water tanks and plant. | https://www.bisnow.com/news/new-york/office/wework-chelsea-full-building-lease-18-west-18th-st-85587 |
| marqeta | 180 Grand Ave, Oakland CA (1981) | Fifteen-storey office slab by Lake Merritt with a parking garage behind it. | https://ellispartners.com/ellis-partners-acquires-180-grand-avenue-oakland/ |
| idme | 8280 Greensboro Dr, Tysons VA (1985) | Ten-storey office slab with a stepped plan, precast bands and ribbon glass. | https://skyscraperpage.com/b110877 |
| chainalysis | 114 Fifth Ave, Flatiron, New York (Maynicke & Franke, 1909) | Eighteen-storey Beaux-Arts loft tower on its corner, rising well above its lower neighbours. | https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/114-fifth/16239 |
| wellsfargo | 420 Montgomery / 464 California St, San Francisco: the HQ from 1959 to 2024 | Twelve-storey 1959 modernist slab running through the block, with the low former museum and banking-hall wing in front. | https://sfstandard.com/2025/10/01/redco-wells-fargo-san-francisco/ |
| norwich | Upper Parade Ground, Norwich University, Northfield VT (education) | Long parade lawn framed by red-brick barracks and halls. Jackman Hall, with its portico and cupola, closes one end; the steepled White Memorial Chapel is one block over. | https://guides.norwich.edu/miningforold/home/From-the-Reading-Room-Upper-Parade-Ground-Origin-Story |

## Uncertain details

These were modelled generically:
- facade materials for WeWork, Marqeta, ID.me and Wells Fargo
- the height of the Palantir base: sources give four or six storeys (four is used)
- the storey counts of the individual Norwich barracks, which are about 3–4
